# Support

Myrlet is self-hosted infrastructure for developers and technical teams.
Community support is handled through GitHub; there is no commercial support
channel or response-time commitment.

## Before asking

1. Check the [documentation index](docs/README.md), especially the [developer quickstart](docs/development/getting-started.md), [deployment guide](docs/deployment/README.md), and [CI and verification guide](docs/development/ci.md).
2. Check existing [Issues](https://github.com/myrlet/myrlet/issues) for known limitations and ongoing work.
3. Open a new [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/myrlet/myrlet/issues/new) with reproduction details or your proposal.

Please include the Myrlet commit or version, deployment mode, relevant logs with secrets removed, reproduction steps, and the expected versus observed behavior. Do not include credentials, tokens, private user data, or unredacted production logs.

## Security reports

Do not use public Issues for vulnerabilities or security-sensitive details. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use the private reporting path described there.

## Scope

The issue tracker is the public support channel for project defects and development proposals. It is not an emergency response service, managed hosting service, or guarantee of compatibility with arbitrary harnesses, platforms, or execution backends.
